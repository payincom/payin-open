## Product Family

PayIn Open is the open-source payment gateway in the PayIn product family.

PayIn Go is a separate face-to-face payment product for in-person merchants.

## Recommended First Step

Start with sandbox/testnet. Verify payment creation, payment page rendering, blockchain monitoring, and webhook delivery before preparing production.
